# Asset licences

Every model, animation, texture and sound in Wildfront is generated by this repository's own code:

- `art/blender/` (Blender Python generators) produces `assets/models/**`.
- `art/palette/make_palette.py` produces the starting `assets/palette/palette.png` (repainted by hand later).
- `client/ui/icons.gd` and `client/sfx.gd` draw the placeholder icons and synthesise the placeholder sounds at runtime.

No third-party assets, asset packs or AI-service outputs are used.
